Meet your coach

Why Hundreds of Men Trust Ian With Their Transformation

A decade competing as a national-level bodybuilder in Canada, certifications in training and nutrition, and a fitness meal-delivery business. Then I traded Canadian winters for the coast of Vietnam.

Hitting 40 changed everything. A slower metabolism and nagging injuries forced my training to evolve: mobility, recovery and precision nutrition. The result is a physique that's leaner, stronger and healthier than in my competitive years, and it became Mind Muscle Training.

My motivation is my son, Daniel. I train to lead by example and show up as the best version of myself for the life we're building together.
